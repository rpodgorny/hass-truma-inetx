- the **"iNet X" wordmark was removed**, keeping only the graphical mark;
- the mark was **re-centred and scaled** on a white circular field, and the
  result exported at 256×256 and 512×512 to meet the
  [Home Assistant brands](https://github.com/home-assistant/brands) icon spec.

No colours, proportions or shapes within the mark itself were altered.

## Why it is here

Home Assistant identifies each integration by the logo of the device or service
it talks to — that is the entire purpose of the `brands` repository, and since
Home Assistant 2026.3 a custom integration may ship the same assets locally.
The mark is used **descriptively**, to identify the Truma iNet X panel this
integration communicates with.
